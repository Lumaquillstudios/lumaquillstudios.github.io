Glowtrail Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This policy explains how the Glowtrail game ("the app") handles information. Glowtrail is published by Lumaquill Studios, a trading name of Kambal Ltd ("we", "us"), registered in England and Wales, company number 17395044.

Summary

Glowtrail does not collect, store or share any personal information. It works fully offline.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress, such as which levels you have unlocked and the stars you have earned, on your own device. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or any account details.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • The app does not use the internet to send any data to us or to third parties.

Google Play

You download Glowtrail through Google Play. Google may collect information about your download and device under its own privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy. We do not receive personal information about you from Google.

Children

Glowtrail is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how the app handles information, such as advertising, we will update this policy and the effective date above before releasing that version.
