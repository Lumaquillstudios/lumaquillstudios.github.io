Puzzlume Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how the Puzzlume app ("the app"), including its games Missing Piece, Glow Link, Odd Glow and Glowtrail, handles information. Puzzlume is published by Lumaquill Studios, a trading name of Kambal Ltd ("we", "us"), registered in England and Wales, company number 17395044.

Summary

Puzzlume does not collect, store or share any personal information. It works fully offline.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress, such as your levels, stars, coins, daily reward streak and sound setting, on your own device. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or any account details.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • The app does not use the internet to send any data to us or to third parties.

Device features

The app may use your phone's vibration for short feedback when you play. You can turn sound and vibration off on the app's home screen. This does not involve any personal information.

Google Play

You download Puzzlume through Google Play. Google may collect information about your download and device under its own privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy. We do not receive personal information about you from Google.

Children

Puzzlume is designed for players aged 13 and over. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how the app handles information, such as advertising, we will update this policy and the effective date above before releasing that version.
